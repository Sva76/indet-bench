# AI assistance and human responsibility

AI tools (including ChatGPT) assisted this project with research planning, discussion of experimental protocols, notebook and Python code, data-processing suggestions, statistical interpretation, drafting, and documentation.

The project owner reviewed experimental decisions and executed model experiments, and participated in human semantic/classification reviews. **Not all human reviews were independent of AI assistance or blinded to all study materials.** This distinction must be preserved in scientific reporting. AI assistance is not independent scientific validation.

Versioned protocols, exact analysis code, frozen data hashes, audit discrepancies, exclusions, and model-specific limitations must accompany scientific claims.

Before any public release, verify that this disclosure accurately describes the full dated record and the applicable publication policies.

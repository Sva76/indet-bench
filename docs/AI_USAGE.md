# Use of AI tools in IndetBench

*Last updated 18 November 2026.*

IndetBench was conceived and directed by Simona Vargiu as principal investigator. She defined the research question and the experimental design, made every scientific decision, ran the experiments and validated their outputs. Implementation was delegated to AI tools: **all Python code in the repository was generated by AI tools to her specifications**, and she checked it by executing it and examining its results. This page sets out what each tool did, what the principal investigator did, and what this means for how the results should be read.

## What the principal investigator did

- Formulated the research question and the B/C determinability design.
- Chose the models, tests and comparisons. For example, the models of the 14 November run, as recorded in [`notebooks/Note_modelli_nuovi_v3.md`](../notebooks/Note_modelli_nuovi_v3.md).
- Approved protocols, thresholds and reading rules before the data they apply to. Decisions are recorded as "owner decision" in commit messages and documents.
- Executed the model experiments and scripts, including the Colab GPU runs, and inspected their outputs.
- Took part in the human semantic and classification reviews.
- Approved the reports marked as "owner-approved". Where a report gives a different verdict from the code, the owner's verdict is stated explicitly. An example is Yi-1.5-9B-Chat, classified "not evaluable" in [`results/new_models_2026-11-14/README.md`](../results/new_models_2026-11-14/README.md).

## How the code was checked

The principal investigator checked the AI-generated code by running it and examining what it produced. The checks used were:

- the unit tests in `tests/` and the CI workflows;
- replay of the archived original results with zero differences (Test C 508 values, Test M notebook A 498, notebook B 176; see [REPRODUCIBILITY_STATUS.md](REPRODUCIBILITY_STATUS.md));
- SHA-256 checksums of inputs and archives;
- positive controls, such as Qwen3-8B reproducing all 1600 Test U2 rows in the 14 November run;
- an independent recomputation of the reported statistics with pandas.

**Limits of these checks:**

- The tests, replay scripts and recomputations were themselves written by AI tools. They lower the risk of error but cannot rule out an error shared between the code and its checks.
- No human programmer has reviewed the code line by line (confirmed by the owner, 15 November 2026).

## Limits on independence

- AI assistance is not independent scientific validation.
- Not all human reviews were independent of AI assistance, and not all were blinded to the study materials. This distinction must be preserved in any scientific report. See also [REPRODUCIBILITY_STATUS.md](REPRODUCIBILITY_STATUS.md), item 4.
- Scientific claims must be accompanied by the versioned protocols, the exact analysis code, the frozen data hashes, the audit discrepancies, the exclusions and the model-specific limitations.

## Authorship and responsibility

AI tools are not authors of IndetBench. [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) lists only the owner, who takes full responsibility for the content of the repository, including any errors in AI-generated code or text.

## Suggested disclosure for papers and reports

Adapt the following to the policy of the venue:

> All analysis code for this work was generated with AI tools (ChatGPT, OpenAI; Claude Opus 5.5 via Claude Code, Anthropic) to the author's specifications; the author designed and directed the research. The author executed it and checked its outputs against unit tests, replay of archived results, checksums and positive controls; these checks were also AI-generated, and the code has not been reviewed line by line by a human programmer. AI tools also assisted with protocol design discussions, statistical interpretation and drafting. Not all human reviews were independent of AI assistance. The author takes full responsibility for the content.
